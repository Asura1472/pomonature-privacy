Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

PomоNature ("the App") is developed and provided by Decafsoft. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Personal Information

PomоNature does not collect, store, transmit, or share personally identifiable information. No account registration or login is required to use the App.

2. Local App Data

The App may store information such as timer settings, selected nature sounds, session history, and user preferences locally on your device.

This information is used only to provide the App's features and is not transmitted to Decafsoft or any external server.

3. Internet and Network Communication

The core features of PomоNature do not require personal information to be transmitted over the Internet.

4. Notifications

The App may use system notifications to inform you when focus or break sessions are completed and to support timer-related functionality. Notification permission is used only for these App features.

5. Background Audio

PomоNature may continue playing nature sounds or operating the timer while the App is running in the background or while the device screen is turned off. This functionality does not involve the collection of personal information.

6. Advertising

PomоNature does not display third-party advertisements and does not use advertising identifiers for advertising purposes.

7. Analytics and Tracking

PomоNature does not use third-party analytics services to track user behavior and does not track users across apps or websites.

8. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on standard Android and Google Play platform services required for application distribution and operation. Decafsoft does not independently collect personal information through these services.

9. Children's Privacy

PomоNature does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features or data handling practices change. Any updates will be published on this page.
